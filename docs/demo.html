CydOs

Click around like you would with a finger on the real screen. This runs the same app logic as the firmware - WiFi Radar uses invented demo networks here since a browser tab can't scan real ones.

Drag sliders, tap tiles, hold the dice's ROLL button. Everything here - the radar sweep, the QR codes, the timers - is the actual CydOs behavior, just running in JS instead of on an ESP32.
