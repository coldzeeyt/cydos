The CydOs App Store

Apps and wallpapers other people made for CydOs.

Requires a microSD card in your CYD. The App Store's actual "install" step - SD Card Apps and Wallpapers, below - reads files straight off the card, no reflash needed. If your board doesn't have a card in it, the only way to add an app is the advanced Community Edition path further down (a firmware reflash, no SD card needed).

SD Card Apps (no reflash)

CydOs looks for simple screens on your microSD card at /cydos_apps/*.cydapp every time it boots. These aren't full programs - just a name, a background color, and a few lines of text - but nothing to compile, flash, or even restart more than once.

  1. 1
    Format a microSD card as FAT32 and make a cydos_apps folder on it.
  2. 2
    Create a text file in there ending in .cydapp, e.g. wifi-card.cydapp: 
    name=WiFi Card
bg=#101820
text=WiFi: MyNetwork
text=Pass: hunter2
  3. 3
    Put the card in your CYD and power-cycle it. Your screen shows up as a Home tile.

Editing the card takes effect on the next boot, not live. Up to 6 .cydapp files at once. Full field reference in CONTRIBUTING.md.

Community Edition (advanced, no SD card needed)

For real interactive apps (not just static screens), written in C++ against the same App interface the built-in apps use. These get compiled into a separate, opt-in Community Edition firmware you flash like normal - a bigger commitment than an SD card file, but works on any CYD.

  1. 1
    Implement the App interface (onEnter/update/draw/onTouch - see App.h) in one self-contained header file.
  2. 2
    Fill out the form below and generate a submission - it packages your app's name/author/icon/description and code into a pre-filled GitHub issue.
  3. 3
    Open the issue. A maintainer turns it into a pull request - this is the human review step before any code ends up in something flashed to someone else's hardware.
  4. 4
    Once merged, CI rebuilds CydOsCommunity.bin automatically and it appears below, ready to install with one click.

Prefer to do it yourself with git? See CONTRIBUTING.md for the community-apps/<slug>/app.json format and how to open the PR directly.
